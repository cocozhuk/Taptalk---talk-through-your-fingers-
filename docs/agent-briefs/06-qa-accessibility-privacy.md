# QA, Accessibility and Privacy Brief

## Mission

Demonstrate that TapTalk is reliable, usable, private, and honest about its
limitations.

## Responsibilities

- Convert the product contract into acceptance tests.
- Test lighting, backgrounds, skin tones, hand sizes, camera quality, tremor,
  partial mobility, occlusion, crossed hands, and hand loss.
- Test duplicate suppression, rearming, rapid contacts, and simultaneous input.
- Test English and Mandarin validation and voice routing.
- Measure contact-to-visual and contact-to-audio latency distributions.
- Review keyboard accessibility, contrast, motion, error messaging, and setup.
- Verify local-only processing and configuration storage.
- Identify claims the prototype cannot yet support.

## Boundaries

- Diagnose and document failures before changing implementation.
- Do not redefine product behavior.
- Do not approve a prototype based only on a happy-path demonstration.

## First assignment

Create the risk-based test plan and acceptance matrix immediately, then verify
the narrow proof of concept and the integrated eight-finger prototype as they
become available.
