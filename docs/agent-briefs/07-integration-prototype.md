# Integration and Prototype Brief

## Mission

Turn the specialist outputs into one coherent, runnable TapTalk prototype while
protecting the shared contract and subsystem boundaries.

## Responsibilities

- Inspect the project and choose the smallest suitable prototype architecture.
- Establish shared scaffolding, development commands, and verification.
- Define module boundaries and merge specialist interfaces.
- Integrate tracking, state, UI, storage, and voice subsystems.
- Resolve cross-subsystem incompatibilities.
- Maintain an end-to-end latency budget.
- Deliver the narrow proof of concept before expanding to all eight fingers.
- Keep a known-limitations and release-readiness record.

## Boundaries

- Do not silently redefine specialist behavior.
- Do not expand the product into sign-language or arbitrary-gesture recognition.
- Do not expose additional languages or voices.
- Prefer narrow integration fixes over wholesale subsystem rewrites.

## First assignment

Inspect the empty repository, recommend the prototype stack and structure, and
prepare an integration plan. Begin shared scaffolding only after checking the
Product Contract and recording assumptions. Coordinate the first runnable
prototype from specialist handoffs.
